# Onboarding research: how similar tools welcome someone new

Three subagents each studied one tool: what the first screen is, what it asks for, and how soon you get something useful. Sources are listed under each tool. Where a subagent couldn't see screens directly, its findings come from help centers and reviews.

## 18Birdies (GPS, scoring and stats)
- **First screen:** a sign-in page (phone or email), then name, password and gender. Location and notifications come afterwards. Handicap is never asked for; it's calculated from rounds.
- **Time to value:** after sign-up plus round setup (course, tee, options), roughly 8–12 taps before the first hole. "Preview a round from the couch" lets you try it early.
- **In-round entry:** score → basic stats (fairway, green, putts), with "Add chips, penalties and more" collapsed. A miss takes two taps: X, then a direction. Par 3s skip the fairway question.
- **Bringing people back:** a round summary with handicap trend, best and average comparison, and a "top area to improve" card (Premium) linking to drills. Also social feeds, badges and yearly recaps.

Sources: [account](https://help.18birdies.com/article/15-creating-a-new-account) · [stats](https://help.18birdies.com/article/29-tracking-your-statistics) · [couch preview](https://help.18birdies.com/article/231-preview-a-round-right-from-your-couch) · [round summary](https://help.18birdies.com/article/32-round-summary) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/18birdies-golf-gps-tracker/id892700751)

## Arccos Caddie (sensor-based stats)
- **First screen:** create an account → Bluetooth, camera and mic permissions → profile and handicap → pair sensors club by club (5–10 minutes, "tedious"). You can't start a round until 8 clubs and a putter are paired, and a paid membership is required.
- **Time to value:** strokes-gained breakdown after any round of 6+ holes.
- **Insights:** "Where to Focus" picks the **one** area costing the most strokes (Driving, Approach, Short Game, Putting). You **"Lock it in"**, then get drills sized to 15 / 30 / 60 minutes.
- **Bringing people back:** an AI round summary, progress on the locked goal, handicap updates and a Year in Review.

Sources: [setup guide](https://www.caddiehq.com/resources/how-to-use-arccos-golf) · [review](https://breakingeighty.com/arccos-caddie-review) · [start a round](https://support.arccosgolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/35105433819924-How-do-I-start-a-round) · [2026 update](https://www.arccosgolf.com/blogs/community/arccos-just-dropped-its-biggest-app-update-ever-heres-whats-new) · [demo mode](https://eu.arccosgolf.com/blogs/community/arccos-caddie-app-now-features-a-demo-mode)

### Follow-up: Arccos Air and how Arccos recommends practice
- **What Air is:** a sensorless wearable shot tracker, launched March 18, 2026 ($349.99 including the first year of membership). It only records **on-course** shots and has no range or practice mode. The practice recommendations come from the June 2026 app redesign, which Air feeds data into.
- **How recommendations work:**
  - Strokes Gained in 4 categories is measured against a **goal you pick** (a target handicap, scoring average, or SG benchmark).
  - **"Where to Focus"** names the single area costing the most strokes from a short list. You tap **"Lock it in"**, and drills are generated "to suit the amount of time you have".
  - A **Pre-Round Warm-Up** builds a 15 / 30 / 60-minute range routine from the current focus.
- **The gap:** practice sessions aren't logged. The focus only changes when new rounds come in. One review calls it "diagnosis only… what you do about it at the range is still on you."
- **For Bucket List:**
  - One "Where to Focus"-style card measured against a **chosen target** (bogey golfer by default), showing the gap ("+2.1 strokes a round vs. a 15 handicap"), with the runner-up in small text.
  - **Lock in** pins the focus until you change it, so it doesn't flip after every round.
  - Use **miss directions** for detail Arccos needs sensors for ("most missed greens are short → club-up carry drill").
  - **Feed practice back in**, which Arccos doesn't: a challenge-score trend for the locked focus.
  - Label it **"estimated"**, never "Strokes Gained" or "AI". We only have score, putts and hit/miss, not shot distances.

Sources: [Arccos Air](https://www.arccosgolf.com/products/air) · [launch news](https://pluggedingolf.com/golf-news-march-18-2026/) · [Air review](https://pluggedingolf.com/arccos-air-shot-tracker-review/) · [no range use](https://support.arccosgolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/34916090489748) · [app update](https://www.arccosgolf.com/blogs/community/arccos-just-dropped-its-biggest-app-update-ever-heres-whats-new) · [Golf Wire](https://thegolfwire.com/arccos-app-update/) · [Golf Insider](https://golfinsideruk.com/best-strokes-gained-apps/)

## Break X Golf (practice planning)
- **First screen:** sign up for a free trial, then a few questions about your game ("no stats needed"), weekly practice time and available facilities.
- **Time to value:** about 5 minutes to a plan, plus a **quick-start plan** you can use before entering any stats.
- **Session structure:** warm-up → focused drills → a scored challenge with a target for your handicap. Every session says **why** it was chosen ("Approach 100–125 yds costs ~2.1 shots/round").
- **Bringing people back:** level-ups, a "new plan ready" alert after logging rounds, and a weekly Practice Hub. CORE Golf, for comparison, uses a forgiving weekly "discipline" score instead of streaks.

Sources: [Break X](https://breakxgolf.com/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/break-x-golf/id6744182938) · [review](https://golfinsideruk.com/break-x-golf-review/) · [practice apps roundup](https://golfinsideruk.com/best-golf-practice-apps/)

## What Bucket List uses in its first visit
| Pattern | Borrowed from | In Bucket List |
|---|---|---|
| Value before any data | Break X quick-start | Home offers **Practice now**, a starter range plan that needs no rounds |
| No setup wall | (all three gate it, so we avoid it) | No account, handicap or profile. You can tap in hole 1 within 3 taps |
| Course data comes to you | 18Birdies / Arccos course DBs | Pick a course, and par, yards and handicap fill themselves in |
| Core stats up front, extras collapsed | 18Birdies | Score, putts, fairway, green, with penalties and notes under "More" |
| One focus, locked in | Arccos | The summary names the single biggest leak, then "Lock in as practice focus" |
| Say why | Break X | Every plan shows "Why this plan" with estimated strokes lost |
| Time-sized practice | Arccos | 15 / 30 / 60 minute picker |
| Forgiving return hook | CORE Golf | A weekly goal ("1 of 2 sessions"), not a daily streak |

**Avoided:** accounts or paywalls before value, sensor or permission setup, too many metrics, and cleaning up data after the round.
